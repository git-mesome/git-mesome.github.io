In an era where AI is all the rage, I had a nagging anxiety about falling behind, and I didn't even know what I didn't know. This book turned out to be a good guide through that. Rather than stopping at abstract advice, it laid out concrete practices, which let me identify my own gaps and plan out actual action items.

Through this book, I was able to sketch a bigger picture of how to grow as a professional, not just as a developer or engineer, but as a person. I think it's a good book because it treats AI strictly as a tool and keeps the focus on the fundamentals, ultimately on the engineer's own judgment and insight.
